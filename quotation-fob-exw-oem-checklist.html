How Türkiye importers can compare a wallpaper glue quotation

A low price per bag is not a complete quotation. A buyer should compare the same product scope, sample evidence, packaging configuration, batch documents, commercial basis and handoff point. This matters when one offer covers 20kg raw-material bags, another includes OEM small packs, and a third excludes export or China-side logistics costs.

Short answer: send every supplier one RFQ sheet with the target wallpaper type, pack format, quantity, sample requirement, Turkish artwork status, document list, delivery basis and named destination. Ask the supplier to state inclusions, exclusions, validity and assumptions instead of comparing headline prices alone.

1. Define the product before asking for price

“Wallpaper glue powder” can refer to different finished adhesive grades or to modified-starch raw material used in a formulation. The RFQ should name the intended use, wallpaper types, target mixing instructions, performance checks and whether the buyer wants a finished product or manufacturing input. KARN's China wallpaper-glue supplier selection checklist provides a structured starting point. Do not transfer a raw-material data sheet to a finished adhesive or assume that the number “999” always describes the same item.

2. Compare a sample under controlled conditions

  1. Identify the sample, supplier, formulation/grade reference and date.
  2. Use the ratio from the current label or TDS, not a universal internet value.
  3. Record water source and temperature, powder/water mass, mixing order and rest time.
  4. Test with the target wallpaper and prepared wall surface.
  5. Keep an approved reference sample for the first commercial batch.

The sample route should also state who pays courier charges, which documents travel with the sample and whether the buyer uses a China forwarder. See the sample, courier and forwarder workflow. Sample acceptance is not a blanket guarantee for every wall, climate or application method.

3. Separate 20kg bulk from OEM small packs

ConfigurationQuote fields to confirm
20kg bulkNet weight, kraft outer bag, inner PE liner, bag mark, pallet option, batch identity and handling method.
OEM small packPack size, film/pouch structure, artwork version, barcode, carton quantity, carton mark, print setup and approved sample.
BothMOQ by configuration, production assumptions, inspection, retained sample, document package and change-control method.

For private label, the buyer should approve formula/grade, net weight, packaging, label copy, carton and master sample as separate items. The OEM label and carton-mark workflow explains version control. Turkish mandatory text and regulatory claims require review by the importer and qualified local professionals; a factory artwork proof is not a legal approval.

4. Ask for an itemized commercial quotation

The quote should state currency, quantity, unit basis, packaging, tooling or print setup, pallets, inspection assumptions, payment milestones, production window, validity and tax/export inclusions. If MOQ changes with pack size or artwork, each configuration needs its own line. KARN's quotation checklist helps expose missing fields without presenting a fixed market price or universal MOQ.

5. Compare FOB and EXW on the same route

EXW and FOB are not interchangeable price labels. The named place or port, export clearance, origin handling, loading, documents and forwarder responsibilities must be written into the quote and proforma invoice. The buyer should confirm Incoterms version and obtain logistics advice for the actual shipment. Use the FOB/EXW and China-forwarder checklist to define the handoff point. This technical note is not a contract or Incoterms legal interpretation.

6. Link the quote to shipment documents

Before payment and before shipment, the buyer should know which documents will be issued and which are examples only. Typical records may include a commercial invoice, packing list, batch COA and current TDS/MSDS. Additional certificates depend on the shipment, product identity and destination requirements; they must not be invented or promised before confirmation. The proforma invoice and packing-list workflow shows how commercial fields connect to the physical shipment.

7. Approve the first order as a controlled handover

The first order should retain the approved sample, signed specification, artwork version, pre-shipment photos, batch identifiers and the final document set. Incoming inspection then compares what arrived with those records. A clear change-control process is needed before repeat orders if formula, raw materials, packaging components, print files or production assumptions change. Do not treat one successful batch as proof that every future configuration is automatically identical.

Minimum comparison sheet

  • Product/grade, intended wallpaper types and sample reference.
  • 20kg or OEM pack specification, inner liner, artwork and carton.
  • Quantity, MOQ basis, unit price, setup costs, currency and validity.
  • Payment milestones, production assumptions and inspection point.
  • FOB/EXW named place, forwarder contact and responsibility split.
  • Invoice, packing list, batch COA, current TDS/MSDS and required buyer-side compliance review.

Conclusion

A useful quotation connects the approved product, sample, packaging, documents and logistics into one auditable scope. It prevents buyers from comparing unlike offers and gives the supplier enough information to quote a real configuration. Price, MOQ, production timing and freight remain quotation-specific and should be reconfirmed when the order scope changes.
